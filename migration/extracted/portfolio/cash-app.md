Cash App

2022  - 2024

1.  Led the end-to-end delivery of the Cross-Border Payments feature, leveraging server-driven UI with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, and collaborating closely with design and product stakeholders to address complex payments flows.
    
2.  Specialised in building accessible, WCAG 2.1-compliant design systems, driving the migration from legacy view-based UI to Jetpack Compose, and developing custom components such as toolbars, buttons, and keypads.
    
3.  Actively contributed to talent acquisition and retention through interviews, presentations, and organising team events, while representing the team at showcases, hack weeks, and the Mobile Summit in San Francisco.
    
4.  Design Systems:
    
    -   Specialised in building design systems, with a focus on Jetpack Compose for custom, complex, and accessible UI components.
        
    -   Made impactful technical contributions, resolving obstacles, and leading migrations from legacy view based UI elements to Jetpack Compose for various design system elements. E.g Toolbar, Buttons, Custom Layouts such as Keypad and Amount views.
        
    -   Proposed and developed a Storybook/demo android app for the Design System, serving as a single source of truth.
        
5.  Accessibility:
    
    -   Spearheaded A11y culture by building a11y technical tooling, documentation guides and testing frameworks which helped the teams across the org. to build accessible features.
        
    -   Built custom layouts to support accessibility requirements for the Cash App.
