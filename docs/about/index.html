About

I'm Karan Sharma, a Staff Software Engineer in Sydney. I have spent more than ten years building secure mobile products, at Commonwealth Bank, Block (Cash App) and Afterpay among others.

At Commonwealth Bank I work across Android and iOS on the CommBank app. I build AI-powered features for the app, and the agentic strategy and AI tooling for mobile across the whole bank. That includes a RAG and a graph RAG that hold all of our mobile engineering knowledge, so engineers can ask a question and get an answer without a meeting. Before that I built the CommBiz business banking app from the ground up, including its architecture, engineering standards and AI strategy. I also train engineers across the org on AI-assisted development.

Outside work I maintain KRAIL, an open source Sydney transit app in Compose Multiplatform, and two small games, Sumi and Huezoo. I care about accessibility, and about software that works for everyone who has to use it. I have spoken about KRAIL at Google I/O Extended, and I write about what I learn.

I studied computer science at Punjab Technical University in India, and started my career in Bangalore before moving to Sydney.
